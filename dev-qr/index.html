Dripn on your phone

Updated 2 Jul 2026 — Metro LAN mode (tunnel unavailable).

If the app “downloads but doesn’t open” — you’re likely on Step 2 before Step 1 is finished, or iOS hasn’t trusted the developer yet. Follow the steps in order.

Step 1 — Install the Dripn dev app (one time)

Scan with iPhone Camera → Safari opens → tap Install. Sign in to Expo if asked.

https://expo.dev/accounts/ahmayatazah/projects/dripn/builds/410d4c88-3dfb-4777-82e7-f93ee4052bdf

After install, before opening:

  1. Settings → General → VPN & Device Management
  2. Tap the developer profile → Trust
  3. Open the Dripn icon from your home screen once (you should see the Expo dev launcher)

Do not use Expo Go — Dripn needs this custom dev build.

Step 2 — Connect to your PC (scan this after Step 1)

Phone and PC must be on the same Wi‑Fi. Tunnel mode is broken on this PC (ngrok error).

exp+dripn://expo-development-client/?url=http://192.168.0.184:8081
