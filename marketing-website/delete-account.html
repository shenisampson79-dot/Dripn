Delete your Dripn account

Dripn is operated by Dripn App Ltd. This page explains how to request deletion of your Dripn account and the personal data Dripn holds for that account. You do not need to sign in to this website to use these instructions.

How to request deletion

You can request deletion in either of these ways:

  1. In the Dripn app: open Settings, go to Account Actions, then tap Delete Account. Confirm the prompts. This permanently deletes your account.
  2. By email: send a deletion request from the email address on your Dripn account to privacy@dripnapp.com. We verify that you own the account before completing deletion.

If you subscribed through an app store, deleting your Dripn account does not cancel that store subscription. Cancel it in the applicable store account settings to avoid future charges. For Apple subscriptions, use Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

What happens to your data

When deletion is completed, Dripn removes your account and associated personal data from live systems. This includes:

  • Your account and profile information
  • Wardrobe items and related images stored for your account
  • Chat and styling history stored for your account
  • Other in-app personal data Dripn stores for your account, such as events, shopping lists and related records

Dripn removes this personal data from live systems when the deletion request is completed. Residual backups are cleared within 30 days.

Data we may need to retain

Some data may be retained where needed for legal, security or fraud-prevention purposes. Anonymized data may be retained for analytics. Payment providers may retain their own billing or transaction records under their terms and applicable law. Dripn does not control those provider records.
