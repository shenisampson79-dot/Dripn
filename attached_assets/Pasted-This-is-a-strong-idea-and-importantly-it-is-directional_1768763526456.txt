## 3️⃣ Is “1 post per day” for free users wise?

### Short answer

**Yes — but only if it’s framed correctly and constrained properly.**

### Why 1 per day works

* Encourages daily habit
* Prevents spam / gaming
* Feels generous but limited
* Creates a clear contrast with “unlimited”

### What NOT to do

* Don’t allow:

  * Multiple photos
  * Long threads
  * Iteration loops
* Don’t show “You have 0 left” aggressively

### Better UX framing

Instead of:

> “You’ve used your post for today”

Say:

> “I’ll help again tomorrow — or anytime with a subscription.”

Calm. Authoritative. No guilt.

## 5️⃣ How this changes the stylist’s mindset (important)

Yes — this feature **does require a stylist mindset shift**, but it’s a good one.

### New stylist role

The stylist is no longer just:

* Creating outfits
* Planning looks

They are now also:

* A **decision referee**
* A **confidence validator**
* A **taste filter**

---

### Stylist behaviour rules for this feature

The stylist should:

* Give **one clear recommendation**
* Explain briefly *why*
* Avoid hedging language
* Avoid “it depends” unless necessary

Example:

> “Go with Option B. It’s more versatile, and you’ll reach for it more often.”

If second opinion is requested:

> “I stand by my first choice. The alternative is trendier, but less you.”

This consistency builds **authority**.
