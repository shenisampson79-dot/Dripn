## 2️⃣ Should DFY Lite be increased to 7 outfits because it lasts 14 days?

You’re right to question this — and the answer is **yes, but with a condition**.

### The real issue is NOT number of outfits

It’s **perceived coverage vs structural value**.

You want DFY Lite to feel:

* Generous
* Complete
* Still clearly inferior to Core

---

### ✅ The correct move

**Increase DFY Lite to:**

* **5–7 outfits**

  * One occasion
  * One capsule
  * High item reuse

### The key constraint (this protects Core)

You must ensure:

* Heavy repetition of items across outfits
* No wardrobe-level understanding
* No “smart recombination” language

Not:

> “Here’s a flexible wardrobe.”

Because Core delivers:

* Breadth (more categories)
* Depth (less repetition)
* Future reuse
* Ongoing intelligence

Even if Lite *touches* 25 items, it never *understands* them.

### Recommended DFY Lite framing

> “Enough outfits so you never repeat a look — without building a wardrobe.”
