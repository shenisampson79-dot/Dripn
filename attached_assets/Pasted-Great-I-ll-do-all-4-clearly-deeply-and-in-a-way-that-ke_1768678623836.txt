1️⃣ EXACT “SECOND OPINION” FLOW (COPY + UX)

This is where most products go wrong. The stylist must stay in control.

⸻

PRE-SIGNUP: User taps “Second opinion”

Stylist response (always first)

Here’s my take: this outfit works because it’s balanced and easy to wear.

If you want, I can also get community input to sanity-check it.

This ensures:
	•	AI remains authoritative
	•	Community is framed as validation, not decision-making

⸻

Then show a choice card (not a modal)

Title:
Get a second opinion?

Option A — Free, no signup
🕒 Community input (30–60 mins)
Microcopy:

Good for reassurance, not urgency.

Option B — Urgent
⚡ I need feedback quickly

No prices. No dark patterns.

⸻

If they choose Option A (pre-signup, slow)
	•	Outfit is posted anonymously
	•	No ETA promise beyond a range
	•	User can close the app
	•	If they return later, results are shown only if they sign up

This creates soft deferred conversion.

⸻

If they choose Option B (urgent)

Transition message (still stylist-led)

Fast feedback needs an active audience.

If you want that, I’ll set this up properly.

CTA:
	•	Create account

No payment yet.

⸻

POST-SIGNUP (first urgent use)

Stylist says:

I’ll aim to get early reactions within ~10 minutes.

I’ll update you as they come in.

Key:
	•	“Aim”, not guarantee
	•	Overdeliver whenever possible

2️⃣ WHO VOTES & HOW IT WORKS (WITHOUT FEELING RANDOM)

Community voting only works if users trust the voters.

⸻

MVP VOTER TYPES (DO NOT OVERBUILD)

Tier 1 — Active users (default)
	•	Recently used app
	•	Have saved outfits
	•	Have interacted with stylist

These users:
	•	Are invested
	•	Give better feedback
	•	Are online

⸻

Tier 2 — Style-aligned users (post-MVP)
	•	Similar preferences
	•	Similar past saves
	•	Similar vibe

Not needed at MVP, but design for it.

⸻

Voting format (simple, fast)

Each voter sees:

“Which outfit works better?”

	•	👍 Yes, wear it
	•	🤔 Not sure
	•	👎 I’d change it

Optional one-tap reason chips:
	•	Too formal
	•	Too boring
	•	Too loud
	•	Looks uncomfortable

No essays. No comments at MVP.

⸻

Output shown to user

Not raw votes.

The stylist summarizes:

“Most people agree this works, but a few felt it was a bit formal.”

This keeps:
	•	Clarity
	•	Authority
	•	No chaos

⸻

3️⃣ WHEN COMMUNITY CAN OVERRIDE THE AI (VERY RARE)

This is non-negotiable.

Rule:

The AI is the primary decision-maker.
Community only overrides in social-risk scenarios.

⸻

Allowed override cases

✅ High-stakes social context
	•	Weddings
	•	Job interviews
	•	First dates
	•	Cultural events

If community strongly disagrees, stylist says:

“I’d still wear this — but given the setting, I’d adjust slightly.”

The AI adapts, not abdicates.

⸻

Disallowed override cases

❌ Everyday wear
❌ Casual outfits
❌ Low-stakes errands
❌ Personal taste conflicts

In these cases:

“This comes down to preference — I’d stick with my original choice.”

This preserves confidence.
