## Q1. If DFY is one use case, what is the duration and what happens to the content?

This is where many apps get muddled — so we’ll be precise.

### 🔑 Core principle

**DFY is a service, not a subscription.**
But the *output* of that service must still obey product rules.

---

### ✅ DFY Lite (£19.99) — *One-use-case, time-limited, non-compounding*

**Duration**

* Access window: **14 days**

  * Enough to use outfits
  * Not long enough to feel permanent
* After 14 days:

  * Outfits are **view-only**
  * Editing, regeneration, and reuse are locked

**Saving outfits**

* Yes, users **can save** outfits
* BUT:

  * Saved outfits are **static images/cards**
  * Items are **not extracted into a reusable wardrobe**
  * The stylist does *not* learn from them long-term

Think of it as:

> “A styled lookbook, not a wardrobe.”

This avoids user anger while preserving upgrade incentive.

---

### ✅ DFY Core (£39–£49) — *Persistent wardrobe intelligence*

**Duration**

* Permanent
* Items live in the user’s wardrobe
* Stylist can reference them forever (with subscription)

**Saving outfits**

* Full save
* Editable
* Re-mixable
* Used for future advice

---

### ❌ What you should NOT do

* Do **not** delete DFY Lite outfits → causes distrust
* Do **not** let Lite outfits feed the AI memory → destroys Core value
* Do **not** allow Lite regeneration after window ends

---

### Simple user-facing explanation

> “DFY Lite gives you ready-made outfits for a specific moment.
> DFY Core teaches your stylist what you own so it keeps working for you.”

That’s intuitive.

## Q2. During setup — individual item photos vs full outfit photos?

### Short answer:

**You must support both — but with strict rules.**

---

## The correct approach (this is important)

### 🟢 Individual item photos (PRIMARY input)

**Required for:**

* DFY Core
* Any persistent wardrobe
* Accurate mix & match
* AI learning

**Why**

* Clean edges
* Category clarity
* Fabric + colour accuracy
* Enables future recombination

This is **non-negotiable** for Core.

---

### 🟡 Full outfit / mirror selfies (SECONDARY input)

**Allowed for:**

* DFY Lite
* Inspiration
* Context understanding
* Fit feedback

**But with a constraint**

* Outfit photos are treated as:

  * “Reference looks”
  * Not decomposed reliably
* The stylist may ask:

  > “Can you also upload individual items from this outfit?”

---

### 🔴 What you should NOT promise

* Do not promise accurate wardrobe extraction from mirror selfies
* Do not say “just upload outfits, we’ll handle it” (AI isn’t there yet)

---

### Clear in-product copy (use this verbatim if you want)

> **Best results:** Photograph individual items
> **Optional:** Upload full outfits for inspiration or fit feedback

This manages expectations and protects quality.
