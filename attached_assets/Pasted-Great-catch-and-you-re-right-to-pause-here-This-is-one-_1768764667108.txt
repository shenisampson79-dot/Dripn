## First: who can edit during DFY Lite?

### Short answer

👉 **Only the stylist can edit. Not the user.**

This is intentional and important.

---

## Why the user should NOT be able to edit in DFY Lite

DFY Lite is positioned as:

> *“I don’t want to think — just tell me what to wear.”*

If users can:

* Swap items freely
* Remix outfits
* Add/remove pieces

Then DFY Lite quietly turns into:

* A light wardrobe builder
* A pseudo-Core tier
* A support nightmare

That breaks your value ladder.

---

## What “editing” actually means in DFY Lite

### ✅ What IS allowed (Stylist-led)

During the 14-day window, the **stylist** can:

* Replace an item if user says:

  > “I don’t wear heels”
* Adjust formality
* Adjust weather assumptions
* Swap colours within the same capsule
* Regenerate an outfit variation

This feels like:

> “Refinement”, not “construction”.

---

### ❌ What is NOT allowed

* User manually editing outfits
* Drag-and-drop remixing
* Adding new items
* Saving items to wardrobe

---

### How you explain this to users (important)

Use language like:

> “Tell me what feels off — I’ll adjust.”

Not:

> “Edit your outfit.”

This reinforces the **stylist-as-authority** dynamic.

---

## What about DFY Core?

Different story.

### In DFY Core:

* User **can** edit outfits
* Items are modular
* Stylist learns from changes
* This reinforces the upgrade logic
