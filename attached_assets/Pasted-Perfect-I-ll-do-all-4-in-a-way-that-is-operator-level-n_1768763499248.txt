## ✅ What Community Voting *is*

Community voting is:

Only after **one of these signals**:

* User has asked the stylist ≥ 5 decisions

# 3️⃣ Failure States (this is where trust is won or lost)

You *must* design these upfront.

---

## A. Bad photo quality

If image is unclear:

> “I can’t see this clearly enough to make a confident call.
> Try one clean photo — natural light works best.”

Never guess. Authority means knowing when not to decide.

---

## B. Too many options

If user uploads 4+ items:

> “Give me your top two or three — too many options makes decisions worse.”

You are teaching better behaviour.

---

## C. No context at all

If user provides nothing:

Stylist still answers:

> “Based on what I can see, choose Option A.
> If you want a sharper call, add where you’re wearing this.”

Never block.

---

## D. Contradictory constraints

Example: “Formal wedding” + gym trainers

> “These don’t match the setting.
> If comfort matters more than formality, say the word.”

Calm correction, not judgment.
