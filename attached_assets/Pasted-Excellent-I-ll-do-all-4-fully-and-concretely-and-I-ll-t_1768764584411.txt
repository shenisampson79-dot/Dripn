# 1️⃣ WHEN & HOW TO NOTIFY USERS ABOUT VOTES

### Core principle

**Notifications should reduce anxiety, not create anticipation.**

You are not running DoorDash or WhatsApp.
Silence is often better than noise.

---

## Notification rules (lock these in)

### Pre-signup users

❌ **No push notifications**
❌ No emails
❌ No “come back now” urgency

Why:

* They didn’t commit
* Notifications feel invasive
* Apple reviewers dislike this

**Instead:**
Results are shown **only if they return** — and gated by signup.

This creates *passive conversion*, not pressure.

---

### Post-signup users (non-urgent)

✅ **One notification max**

Trigger:

* Status moves from `partial → complete`

Copy:

> “Your second opinion is ready.”

That’s it. No emojis. No hype.

---

### Urgent requests (post-signup only)

Optional, user-controlled.

Trigger options:

* First reactions in
* Final summary ready

Copy example:

> “Early feedback is in — want to check?”

Important:

* Never more than **2 notifications per request**
* User can mute future updates

---

## What you NEVER notify for

❌ Each individual vote
❌ “Someone disagreed”
❌ “New reaction” spam

You are not Slack.

# 2️⃣ VOTER INCENTIVES (WITHOUT GAMIFICATION)

This is subtle but extremely important.

### Principle

**People vote because it feels useful — not because they earn points.**

---

## MVP incentives (light, invisible)

### 1️⃣ Reciprocity (primary)

If you vote occasionally:

* Your own second opinions get faster responses
* Your requests are prioritised *slightly*

No leaderboard. No explicit promise.

Stylist language:

> “Thanks for helping out — I’ll prioritise your requests when I can.”

That’s enough.

---

### 2️⃣ Taste reinforcement

Voters feel:

> “I have good style judgement.”

This is intrinsic motivation.

You reinforce it quietly:

* “Your input helped someone decide”

No numbers attached.

---

## What to avoid at MVP (hard no)

❌ Points
❌ Badges
❌ Leaderboards
❌ “Top stylist” titles
❌ XP systems

These:

* Attract low-quality votes
* Turn fashion into noise
* Destroy trust

---

## Post-MVP (optional, very light)

If you *must*:

* Private reputation score
* Used only for vote weighting
* Never shown publicly

This keeps quality high without social pressure.

# 3️⃣ HOW “SECOND OPINION” FITS PRICING & TIERS

This is where you monetise **calm reassurance**, not anxiety.

---

## Free (Pre-signup / Free users)

* Can request second opinion
* Slow (30–60 min)
* Low vote count
* No notifications

Purpose:

> Prove the concept exists

---

## Signed-up, Free Tier

* Occasional second opinions
* Standard speed
* No urgency option
* Limited frequency

Purpose:

> Reinforce trust, not dependency

---

## Subscription (£9.99)

This is where it becomes valuable.

Includes:

* Faster vote routing
* Urgent option
* Notifications
* Fewer “no response” cases

You don’t sell it as:

> “More votes”

You sell it as:

> “Fewer moments of doubt.”

---

## DFY tiers

Second opinion is:

* Rarely needed
* Mostly redundant

Why?
Because confidence is higher.

This is good:

> DFY reduces reliance on social validation.

# 4️⃣ TECHNICAL PRD (ENGINEER-READY)

This is intentionally clean and scoped.

---

## Feature: Second Opinion (MVP)

### Goal

Provide optional community validation without undermining AI authority or requiring high DAU.

---

## Functional Requirements

### User

* Can request second opinion on an outfit
* Can choose normal or urgent (post-signup only)
* Receives summarized feedback via stylist

---

### System

* Creates `SecondOpinionRequest`
* Routes to eligible voters
* Collects lightweight votes
* Determines completion state
* Generates stylist summary

---

## Non-Goals (explicit)

* No comments
* No messaging
* No profiles
* No public feeds
* No real-time chat

---

## Vote Routing Logic (MVP)

Eligible voters:

* Recently active users
* Not overloaded
* Optional style alignment later

Routing cap:

* Max 15 users notified
* Stop once threshold reached

---

## Failure Handling

| Case         | Behaviour                 |
| ------------ | ------------------------- |
| No votes     | AI stands by decision     |
| Split votes  | AI reframes & decides     |
| Low DAU      | Feature degrades silently |
| User offline | Results shown on return   |

---

## Analytics to Track

* % of users requesting second opinion
* Conversion after second opinion
* Time to first vote
* AI vs community disagreement rate
* Retention impact

If this doesn’t move retention, you cut or shrink it.
